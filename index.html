A staged, risk-controlled rollout timeline designed to ensure zero academic disruption and total stakeholder alignment.

  • Elected Student Council Charter endorsement
  • Baseline hygiene & mess satisfaction audit
Governance Setup
  • Project Sponsor & PPM Lead appointed
  • Student Mess Committee election framework
  • Risk management register initial baselining
Key Deliverables

Phase 2: Policy Formulation, Tendering & Transparent Pricing (M2–M4)

Procurement Gate

Publishing the transparent rent schedule, legal SLA frameworks for housekeeping & security contractors, and competitive caterer tendering.

Risk Governance

Risk Management Register

Proactive mitigation protocols addressing top project threats identified in the PPM assessment.

Risk 01: Contractor / Renovation Delays Likelihood: Med | Impact: High

Mitigation: Strict liquidated damages penalty clauses in contracts; work staged during semester breaks with dual-shift labor buffers.

Risk 02: Student Resistance to New Policies Likelihood: Low | Impact: High

Mitigation: Co-design policies with student union; transparent town halls; guarantees of 15% rent support for eligible students.

Risk 03: Food Quality Lapses / Vendor Default Likelihood: Low | Impact: Critical

Mitigation: Performance-based payments with 20% retainage; pre-qualified backup caterers on standby; immediate breach cancellation clauses.

Risk 04: Budget Overruns / Inflation Likelihood: Med | Impact: Med

Mitigation: Dedicated 10% contingency reserve locked in escrow; fixed-price turnkey equipment procurement contracts.
